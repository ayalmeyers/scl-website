Leadership Counsel

SCL provides a confidential sounding board for leaders to clarify and align their thinking, actions, and communication to defined strategic objectives.

"People will forget what you said. People will forget what you did. But people will never forget how you made them feel." — Maya Angelou

Through periodic one-on-one conversation and coaching, we challenge and advise leaders to:

  • Clarify their goals
  • Understand their constituents
  • Consider their organizational and marketplace landscape
  • Shape their strategies
  • Define and prioritize actions
  • Establish measurement criteria for evaluating results

This is not a one-time intervention. Leadership Counsel is an ongoing relationship — a place to think out loud, pressure-test decisions, and stay honest about progress against the objectives that matter most.
