Listen to be Heard

SCL Musings

Effective communication requires reciprocal listening. Developing your speaking skills — clarity, engagement, coherence — helps you convey a message. But true persuasiveness depends on being heard generously by others. And to receive generous listening, you must first listen generously to others.

That means:

  • Demonstrating genuine comprehension of what others truly communicate, including what goes unspoken.
  • Reading between the lines and the pauses to understand underlying tension and intention.
  • Setting aside personal assumptions, biases, and agendas.
  • Showing an authentic desire to build mutually beneficial understanding.
  • Maintaining genuine curiosity and openness.

"To be heard, first be willing to hear. To be listened to, first be willing to demonstrate you are listening."

Authentic listening creates the foundation for being truly heard and understood by others — a fundamental principle for effective leadership communication, and one that no framework or technique can substitute for.
