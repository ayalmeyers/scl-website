Privacy

How we handle your information.

What this form collects

When you submit the contact form on this site, we collect the information you enter: your name, email, phone number (optional), company (optional), the reason for your inquiry, and your message.

How it's used

Submissions are delivered by email to Strategic Communication Lab's team, who use them only to respond to your inquiry. We don't sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Where it's processed

Form submissions are delivered using a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit) that relays your message to our email; it does not otherwise use your information.

Requesting deletion

To request that we delete information you've submitted, email admin@scl.us.com.
