Creating Persuasive Messages: The "RPE" Model

Every persuasive message orbits one key message, tested against three questions: is it relevant to this audience, does it make the point directly, and is it backed by evidence?

The D'RACT Model

Most teams lead from the bottom up, letting current thinking and available resources dictate the result. D'RACT reverses that — direction and results first, then the activities, competencies, and thinking needed to deliver them.

The PCRC Model
