## Done this pass (verified by test)

### Round 4 (follow-up)

- **Built the headshot-normalization pipeline**: `scripts/normalize-headshot.sh <input> [output]` (ImageMagick) re-encodes a photo to a consistent JPEG quality (85) and color space (sRGB), strips all embedded metadata (EXIF/ICC/XMP — camera and sometimes location data had no business shipping to site visitors), auto-corrects orientation, and caps the longest edge at 1280px (2x the largest size any headshot is ever displayed at: the bio dialog photo, `min(92vw, 640px)` wide). It's a repeatable script, documented in `README.md`, meant to be run on any new or replaced team photo going forward.
- **Ran it on all 7 current team headshots.** Source files varied wildly — square, portrait, even one landscape; quality settings from 80 to 99; one (`JimZarvos.jpg`) carrying ~23KB of embedded profile/EXIF data for a 576×864 photo. Normalizing brought total size for the 7 headshots from 729KB to 385KB (47% smaller), with the worst offender (`JimZarvos.jpg`) dropping from 346KB to 58KB. No photo was re-cropped or re-composed — only re-encoded — so how each person is framed is unchanged.
- Added `photoWidth`/`photoHeight` to each person's entry in `team.js` (the post-normalization pixel dimensions) and wired them into `renderTeam()`'s three `<img>` renders (homepage teaser, roster card, bio dialog) as explicit `width`/`height` attributes, so the browser can reserve the right space before the image loads.
- **Also ran it on the four other oversized site photos** that showed the same bloat pattern (`01.jpg`, `02.jpeg`, `03.jpg`, `leader-portrait.jpg` — the "Coaches/Counsel/Consultants" pillar photos and a video poster). `01.jpg` dropped from 252KB to 84KB, `03.jpg` from 310KB to 49KB (both were quality-99 JPEGs with no reason to be). `02.jpeg` and `leader-portrait.jpg` were already reasonably encoded and came out roughly flat. Did not touch their resolution otherwise (see "upscaling" note below — that's a separate, decision-blocked issue, not something compression fixes).
- Verified with: `node --check` on `main.js`/`team.js`, the existing roster Playwright test (grid rendering, dialog open/close, bio content, deep-link-on-fresh-navigation all still correct), a full regression sweep (all pages × 3 viewports, console/network/overflow — clean), and before/after screenshots confirming no visible quality loss on any of the 11 re-encoded images.
- Updated `README.md`'s "Editing the roster" section (it was stale — written before the Round 3 `team.js` refactor landed, still describing hand-duplication that no longer exists) and added a "Headshots" section documenting the new script.
