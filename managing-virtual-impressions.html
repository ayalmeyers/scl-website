Managing Virtual Impressions: The 5 Cs

SCL Musings

"What you say is important, but how you come across when you say it matters even more."

In today's digital environment, the capacity to persuade and create meaningful impact has become increasingly critical. Without proper attention to how one presents oneself online, virtual platforms can create negative impressions that harm client relationships. Handled strategically, however, virtual presence can become a genuine competitive advantage.

The professionals who show up best on screen aren't simply the most polished — they are the most deliberate. They have thought through how their setting, their pacing, their eye line, and their energy translate through a lens, and they treat that translation as seriously as they treat the substance of what they're saying.

This is the heart of the 5 Cs framework: a way of auditing virtual presence so that nothing about the medium works against the message. As remote and hybrid work has become permanent rather than temporary, the leaders who invest in this skill are the ones whose virtual meetings convert into real trust, real decisions, and real results.
